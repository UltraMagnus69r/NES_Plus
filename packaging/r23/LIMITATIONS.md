# R2.3 Design Language 2.0

This archive is a test candidate. It is not an accepted build.

The visual system is Design Language 2.0. UnMaker Lab and Maker Engine share tokens and use different surfaces. Discover is a wrapping vertical gallery. A reconstructed Goomba is one card. Walk and Defeat / Stomped are states in Review. There is one Approve & Save Goomba button. Review colors are not a verified NES palette.

Mario, Luigi, and other candidates are not reconstructed. The Maker course starts empty. 16-bit, Modern Pixel, and Retro Palette are shown and cannot be selected. They do not convert art. Move, course zoom, a pan tool, and a snap toggle are visible and disabled.

The verified SMB1 Goomba is still tiles 0x70–0x73, with the second walk pose mirrored, and defeat from tile 0xEF. Package pictures still keep every frame.

Phase 9.1 is not accepted. Phase 10 is not included. R3 has not started. The application starts in a decorated window. Fullscreen is optional.
